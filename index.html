How GLA began

A son's promise to his father turned into one of Mathura's biggest universities.

The dream

Late Shri Ganeshi Lal Agrawal dreamed of a quality institution that would serve the higher education needs of the region and beyond. The university carries his name: GLA.

1998: the founding

His son, Shri Narayan Das Agrawal, now the Chancellor, set up the Sri Jagannath Prasad Ganeshi Lal Bajaj Charitable Trust Samiti and started GLA Institute of Technology and Management to make Mathura a destination for knowledge seekers.

2010: university status

The institute was accorded university status under the U.P. State Legislative Act of 2009 (UP Act 21 of 2010).

2017: NAAC 'A' grade

The university earned an 'A' grade from NAAC.

2023 onwards: NAAC 'A+'

Today GLA holds an 'A+' grade from NAAC and is home to more than 15,500 students.

Sources: gla.ac.in (About Us, RAMSIA pages) and the university's public self-disclosure.
